One typeface in the app, Plus Jakarta Sans, in five sizes. Buttons and tags are full pills, cards use 32px rounded corners, days use 16px, shadows are tinted rose instead of grey, and icons come from Material Symbols Rounded. The selected state is always pink with wine text.
