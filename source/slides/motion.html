The orb has three jobs. On Calm it paces your breathing. On Check-in it mirrors your feeling. On Sessions it gains one small satellite for each day you pick. Anxious and Frustrated start fast, then slow down after 3.5 seconds, so the app soothes instead of mirroring distress. With reduce motion on, the orb stays still.
