These were my own hurdles. Finding the right inspiration took a while. I wanted a mental health app that looks and works like a period tracker calendar: soft, simple and something you open every day. I found a Dribbble concept with that feel and built my blush look from it. I was attached to my 3D cloud mascot, but it looked like every other AI mascot, so I let it go for the breathing orb. When I tested the app myself, the back buttons sent me to different places, so I made every back button go Home.
