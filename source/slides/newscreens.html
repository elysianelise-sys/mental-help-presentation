19

Three screens were added after I tested the app. Profile now shows my own numbers: check-ins, day streak, saved sessions, this week’s mood, recent check-ins and my booked plan. The Calendar opens from the Home calendar button or from Profile, and shows a month of check-ins with the most recent day selected. Settings was split out of Profile and opens from the gear: reduce motion, rain sounds by default, clearing check-ins and an About note that links back to this presentation.
